The development history of our enterprise PDF platform. Every release is code-signed and timestamped, built for secure, on-premise deployment.

1.6.0 Current

Arabic searchable PDF, copy quality, and a multilingual interface

Our most comprehensive release — focused on document recognition and multilingual use.

  • NewArabic (right-to-left) searchable PDF. Scanned Arabic documents are now searchable and copyable, with text that comes through correctly in standard viewers.
  • NewXML → PDF generation. Turn XML files into PDFs using ready-made templates (General, Invoice, Report, Receipt) — including e-invoices.
  • ImprovedSearchable-PDF copy quality. Text copied from Turkish and English documents now preserves full line integrity.
  • ImprovedMultilingual interface. Added German, Spanish, French, and Portuguese interface support.
  • ImprovedUninterrupted conversions. Every conversion runs in the background — keep working with other documents while a job runs.
  • FixOCR language selection. The chosen document language is now applied correctly in every case.
